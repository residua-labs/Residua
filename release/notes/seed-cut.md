# 0.3 seed cut

The 0.3 release pins one cut of `main`: `fc0fb23a3a` (contains `d533e0ff3b`
— the committed seed's front end reads `rsdc/Compile.rsd` again — and
`0b55499f61` — the narrow column of the two-route Koka table). No further
merges of `main` go into this release.

From this cut the WebAssembly route compiles with the `narrow` layer as its
default; the native route stays 64-bit. The cut seed directory replaces
`bootstrap/seed/stage-14274/` as the seed of record; its README, manifest,
hashes, and stage entries are generated by `bootstrap/seed/Cut.rsd` from the
check output of `bootstrap/seed/Check.rsd`, not written by hand.

After the cut, `lean --run benchmarks/koka/KokaBase.rsd images 30658 <the
seed compile command>` writes `benchmarks/koka/modules.tsv` and
`benchmarks/koka/images.tsv`; module digests equal to the pins in
`reports/koka-table-narrow/` (repository-side) mean the Koka table carries over unchanged.
